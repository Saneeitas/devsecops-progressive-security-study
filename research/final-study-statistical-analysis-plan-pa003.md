# PA-003 final statistical analysis plan

**APPROVED — PA-003 — October 10, 2026**

Source-document provenance: explicit PI declaration in the ChatGPT research conversation on October 10, 2026; based on the revised PA-003 package and approved-candidate schedule.

Total pipeline duration in seconds is the primary outcome. The six directed contrasts are C1−C0 through C6−C5. For each block and adjacent contrast, estimate the mean of the 20 paired duration differences.

Construct two-sided simultaneous 95% Student-t intervals with 19 df and Bonferroni family-wise alpha 0.05 across six contrasts: t(1 − 0.05/(2×6),19) × SD(paired differences)/sqrt(20). Use the paired-difference standard deviation, not independent configuration standard deviations.

The primary analysis requires 20 complete blocks. Valid long observations remain. Fixed-effect block/period, nonparametric paired, bootstrap, temporal/order, environmental, and first-block evaluations are sensitivity/descriptive only.
